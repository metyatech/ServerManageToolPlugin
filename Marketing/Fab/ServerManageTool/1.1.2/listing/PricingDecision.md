# Pricing Decision

## Recommended Fab price

| License tier | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Personal | USD 9.99 |
| Professional | USD 29.99 |

Use the Fab Standard License. The prices are base prices in USD; Fab may apply taxes or VAT.

## Basis

- Fab requires a Personal and a Professional price for the Standard License.
- The selected prices are within the product’s small, focused editor-plugin scope.
- The Personal tier is for Fab’s Personal license audience; Professional is priced separately for commercial teams.
- The public source repository and public prebuilt release files improve inspection and onboarding, but they do not grant an open-source license or replace the Fab buyer license.
- The package includes three modules, two Blueprint-callable functions, packaged documentation, and verified Win64 binaries.
- The product solves a defined local PIE workflow: map-specific addresses, all-port preflight, all-or-nothing launch, runtime port validation, and managed cleanup.

## Market comparison

The accompanying CompetitivePricingResearch.md records a public Fab sample of Network & Multiplayer and Engine Tools listings. Several pages expose Free, while paid pages in the fetched public view expose a license selector without a numeric price. Those pages are recorded as not publicly displaying a comparable numeric price rather than being assigned an inferred price.

This is directional positioning, not a claim that the sample is a complete market survey.

## Price display and promotions

- Base currency: USD.
- No launch discount is proposed in this listing copy.
- Do not describe the public release ZIP as a free or open-source alternative.
- Review the prices when the verified engine matrix, support scope, module count, or Fab license rules materially change.

## Review triggers

Revisit the price if any of the following occurs:

- The plugin adds a TargetType.Server executable or production deployment workflow.
- The plugin adds additional engine-version binaries or platforms.
- The plugin adds substantial runtime networking, replication, hosting, or online-service functionality.
- Support becomes a paid service or includes guaranteed response times.
- Fab changes the Standard License price rules or tier definitions.
